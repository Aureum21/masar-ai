## Scope decisions

- Mall facilities do not establish access to Ground Control or SOCO activities. No activity participation claim was added.
- Corniche details apply to the designated visually impaired beach area near Gate 3, with eligibility and arrangements to be confirmed.
- The Umm Suqeim pathway report does not establish access across all of Sunset Beach or into the water.
- Al Fahidi is described as mostly flat; that does not establish step-free entry to each building.
- Dhayah Fort has an explicit negative step-free fact for the hilltop climb.
- A general wheelchair-access statement does not establish adapted toilets, lifts, walking distances or low-sensory arrangements.
- Wheelchair loans are described under the relevant staff contact point, not as proof of step-free access.
- Museum of the Future facility reports do not establish current opening status.
- The 2024 SMA restroom report is identified as such in its note; the checked date is the date the source was reviewed, not a physical inspection.
