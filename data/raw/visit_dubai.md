# Visit Dubai — Comprehensive Knowledge Base & RAG Manual

**Source:** Visit Dubai (Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism), GDRFA, ICP, Dubai Customs, RTA

This document serves as an exhaustive knowledge base for AI agents handling queries related to Dubai tourism, visas, transportation, local culture, neighborhoods, and top attractions.

## 1. Standard Tourist Visas & Entry Permits

### 1.1 Single-Entry Tourist Visa

A foreigner is granted an entry visa for a single visit for tourism purposes for a period of **30 or 60 days**. The sponsor/host in the UAE must be a registered tourism establishment.

- **Expected Completion Time:** 48.0 Hours
- **Requirements:** One personal photograph, copy of the passport (valid 6+ months), and identity card from the country of origin for specific nationalities (_Iraq, Pakistan, Iran, Afghanistan_).
- **Terms:** Must have a confirmed onward travel ticket and valid medical insurance inside the UAE.
- **Service Fees:** 30-Day: AED 252 + 5% VAT | 60-Day: AED 352 + 5% VAT
- _Inside UAE Surcharges:_ AED 500 (Status Change Fee) + AED 10 (Knowledge Dirham) + AED 10 (Innovation Dirham).
- **Application Link:** GDRFA Smart Channels

### 1.2 Multiple-Entry Tourist Visa

A foreigner is granted a visit multi-entry visa for tourism for a period of **30 or 60 days**.

- **Requirements & Terms:** Identical to the Single-Entry Visa.
- **Service Fees:** 30-Day: AED 300 + 5% VAT | 60-Day: AED 500 + 5% VAT

### 1.3 Multi-Entry Visit Visa (Relative or Friend)

A multi-entry visa granted to visit a relative or friend without a host/guarantor for **30, 60, or 90 days**.

- **Requirements:** Photograph, passport copy (6+ months validity), marriage/birth certificate (to prove relationship), and proof of host's income (salary certificate or business contract).
- **Terms:** Visitor must be a relative of a resident or friend of a UAE citizen.
- **Service Fees:** 30-Day: AED 300 | 60-Day: AED 500 | 90-Day: AED 700 (+ 5% VAT).
- **Guarantees & Insurance:** Refundable Financial Guarantee: AED 2,000 (+ AED 60 fees). Health Insurance: AED 40–90 depending on duration.

### 1.4 Visa on Arrival (VoA) & Transit Rules

- **GCC Nationals:** Citizens of Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia do not require a visa and can enter using a national ID.
- **30-Day Free VoA:** Passports from Australia, Brunei, Canada, China, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand, Singapore, UK, USA, etc.
- **90-Day Multiple-Entry VoA:** Most EU states, Argentina, Brazil, Russia, South Korea, Switzerland.
- **Indian Nationals:** 14-day VoA if holding a valid US visitor visa/Green Card, UK, or EU residence permit (minimum 6 months validity).
- **Transit Visas:** 48 hours (free) or 96 hours (paid). Must be booked via UAE airlines (Emirates, flydubai) prior to arrival.

## 2. Specialized & Long-Term Visas

### 2.1 5-Year Multiple-Entry Tourist Visa

A self-sponsored visa allowing visitors to enter multiple times over 5 years.

- **Stay Duration:** Up to 90 days per visit (extendable by 90 days). Maximum stay is 180 days per calendar year.
- **Financial Requirement:** Applicant must provide a bank statement showing a balance of $4,000 USD (or equivalent) for the last 6 months.
- **Other Requirements:** Health insurance valid for the UAE, flight ticket, and proof of accommodation.

### 2.2 Dubai Remote Work Visa (Digital Nomad)

A 1-year visa allowing foreign professionals to live in Dubai while working for an employer overseas.

- **Cost:** AED 2,300 (approx. $620 USD) for government processing fees (excluding health insurance and document attestation). Renewable annually.
- **Terms:** Applicants must arrange their own UAE health insurance. They can sponsor spouses and dependents once the visa is active and a residential lease (Ejari) is secured.

## 3. Customs, Medications & Border Rules

The UAE strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy on prohibited substances (Federal Decree-Law No. 30 of 2021).

### 3.1 Medication & Drugs

- **Prohibited (No Entry):** Cannabis, CBD oil, recreational drugs, and certain synthetic narcotics.
- **Controlled / Semi-Controlled:** Common prescription medicines containing codeine, tramadol, benzodiazepines, and amphetamine-based compounds are heavily restricted.
- **Requirement:** Tourists carrying controlled medications **must** apply for an import permit from the **Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP)** before traveling. Carrying restricted substances without a permit can result in fines (AED 20,000–100,000) or imprisonment.

### 3.2 Prohibited & Restricted Items

- Paan and betel leaves.
- E-cigarettes and electronic hookah (subject to Emirates Authority for Standardization limits).
- Items contradicting Islamic faith and public morals.

## 4. Public Transport, Fines & Mobility

Public transport is managed by the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA). Cash is not accepted directly on transit networks.

### 4.1 The Nol Card System

A contactless smart card used to pay for Metro, Buses, Trams, Marine transport, and RTA Paid Parking.

- **Red Ticket:** Best for occasional/single trips.
- **Silver Card:** Pre-paid card ideal for tourists and frequent travelers.
- **Gold Card:** Grants access to premium Gold Class cabins on the Metro and Tram.

### 4.2 Dubai Metro Rules & Strict Fines

The RTA enforces strict decorum on the Metro, Tram, and Buses. Common fines include:

- **AED 100:** Eating, drinking, or **chewing gum** onboard or on platforms.
- **AED 100:** Sitting in the Gold Cabin without a Gold Nol card, or men sitting in the Women & Children's cabin.
- **AED 100:** Placing feet on the seats.
- **AED 200:** Riding without a valid Nol card or fare evasion.
- **AED 300:** Sleeping in passenger shelters, metro platforms, or prohibited transit areas.
- **AED 500:** Carrying alcohol (open containers) on public transport.
- **AED 2,000:** Using emergency buttons/exits in non-emergency situations.

## 5. Practical Destination Essentials

- **Currency:** UAE Dirham (AED), pegged to the US Dollar at $1 USD = 3.6725 AED.
- **Taxes:** 5% Value Added Tax (VAT). Tax-free shopping refunds are available at DXB/DWC departure terminals via Planet Payment kiosks.
- **Tipping:** 10%–15% is customary in restaurants and for hospitality services.
- **Power:** Type G plugs (British 3-pin rectangular), 220V–240V at 50Hz.
- **Telecom:** Major networks are e& (Etisalat), du, and Virgin Mobile. Visitors aged 18+ receive complimentary tourist SIM cards at immigration.
- **Emergency Contacts:** Police (999), Ambulance (998), Fire (997), Tourist Police (901), DET Helpline (+971 600 55 55 59).

### 5.1 Culture & Decorum

- **Clothing:** Dress modestly in public areas, malls, and historical sites (shoulders and knees should be covered). Beachwear is strictly for beaches and pools.
- **Alcohol:** Available in licensed hotels, restaurants, and lounges. Tourists can buy alcohol from dedicated stores (MMI, African + Eastern) by presenting their original passport. The legal drinking age is 21. Public intoxication is illegal.

## 6. Explore Dubai: Key Neighbourhoods & Landmarks

- **Downtown Dubai:** Home to the Burj Khalifa, The Dubai Mall, The Dubai Fountain, and Dubai Opera.
- **Dubai Marina & JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence):** High-rise waterfront district famous for the Marina Walk, yacht cruises, beachfront dining, and family watersports.
- **Palm Jumeirah:** Man-made island featuring ultra-luxury resorts (Atlantis The Palm, Atlantis The Royal), The Pointe, and Palm West Beach.
- **Old Dubai (Deira & Bur Dubai):** The historical trading hub flanking Dubai Creek. Discover traditional souks (Gold, Spice, Textile) and authentic street food.
- **Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood:** 19th-century wind towers, courtyard cafes, and the Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Centre for Cultural Understanding (SMCCU).
- **Hatta:** Mountain enclave 90 minutes from the city. Ideal for hiking, kayaking in the Hatta Dam, and the Hatta Heritage Village.

### 6.1 Top Attractions

- **Museum of the Future:** Sheikh Zayed Road landmark exploring next-generation science and innovation.
- **Dubai Frame:** A 150m-high glass bridge viewing past, present, and future Dubai in Zabeel Park.
- **Global Village:** Seasonal outdoor cultural, entertainment, and shopping destination.
- **Deep Dive Dubai:** The world's deepest diving pool.
