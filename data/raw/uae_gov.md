## 1. Visa Eligibility & Entry Permits

### 1.1 General Visa Eligibility

- **GCC Citizens:** Citizens of Gulf Cooperation Council countries do not require a visa or permit to enter the UAE.
- **Visa on Arrival (VoA):** Available for citizens of several eligible countries. Passports must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry. Lists of eligible countries vary, so travelers should verify with the UAE embassy prior to travel.
- **Pre-Arranged Visas:** Required for nationalities not eligible for visa-free entry or VoA.

### 1.2 Standard Tourist Visa

A tourist visa is for those not eligible for a visa on arrival.

- **Issuing Authorities:** Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) or the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) – Dubai.
- **How to Apply:** Note that **UAE embassies do not issue tourist visas**. Visitors must apply through:
  - **UAE-based Airlines:** (e.g., Etihad Airways, Emirates, flydubai, Air Arabia). You must be flying with the airline arranging your visa.
  - **Licensed Travel Agents & Hotels:** Can arrange a visa provided you purchase tickets through them and maintain a hotel reservation.

### 1.3 Multiple-Entry 5-Year Tourist Visa

Enables tourists of all nationalities to enter the UAE multiple times on self-sponsorship.

- **Stay Duration:** 90 days per visit (can be extended for an additional 90 days).
- **Requirements:**
  - Proof of a bank balance of $4,000 USD (or equivalent) during the last 6 months.
  - Valid UAE health insurance policy (valid for 180 days).
  - A confirmed onward or return travel ticket.

### 1.4 eVisa for GCC Residents

GCC residents and their companions who are not GCC citizens must apply online for a visa before arriving in the UAE.

- **Validity:** The entry permit is valid for 30 days from issue. Allowed stay is 30 days from entry (extendable once for 30 days).
- **Conditions:**
  - GCC residency must be valid for at least 1 year from the arrival date.
  - Passport must be valid for at least 6 months.
  - The applicant’s profession must be on the approved list (subject to evaluation).

### 1.5 Transit Visas

- **Types:** Issued for either **48 hours** or **96 hours**.
- **Conditions:** It is a single-entry visa and cannot be extended. Arranged via UAE-based airlines.

## 2. Getting to the UAE

### 2.1 By Air

The UAE is well-connected globally. Key airlines include:

- **Etihad Airways:** The UAE's national airline (Abu Dhabi).
- **Emirates & flydubai:** Fly from and to Dubai International Airport.
- **Air Arabia:** Flies from/to Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Ras Al Khaimah.
- **Wizz Air:** Operates low-cost flights from Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

### 2.2 By Road

Visitors can drive into the UAE from the Sultanate of Oman and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

- **Requirements:** Visitors require an international driving permit and valid vehicle insurance. GCC residents can drive using the driving license issued by their residing country.
- **Driving Rules:** Vehicles are driven on the right-hand side. Traffic signs are in English and Arabic.
- **Checkpoints:** Several entry points exist along the borders; some are reserved exclusively for GCC citizens. Must have a correct exit permit from the origin country and a UAE entry visa.

### 2.3 By Sea

Abu Dhabi and Dubai are major stops for international cruise lines. Visitors can arrive through:

- Abu Dhabi Cruise Terminal, Zayed Port
- Mina (Port) Rashid, Dubai
- Ras Al Khaimah Ports

## 3. Where to Stay & Tourist Taxes

### 3.1 Accommodations

Visitors can book everything from modest hotel apartments to opulent resorts in the deep desert or by the sea. Bookings can be made directly or through airline-affiliated agencies (e.g., Etihad Holidays, Emirates Holidays, Dnata Travel).

### 3.2 Taxes and Fees at Tourist Facilities

Hotels and tourist facilities in the UAE may charge one or more of the following taxes (varies by emirate):

- 10% tax on the room rate
- 10% service charge
- 10% municipality fees
- 6% to 10% city tax
- 6% tourism fee

## 4. What to Do: Exploring by Emirate

### 4.1 Abu Dhabi (The Capital)

- **Landscapes:** Borders the Empty Quarter desert featuring _Tel Moreeb_ (the world's tallest sand dune) and Jebel Hafeet (the emirate's highest peak).
- **Al Ain:** Known as the Oasis City. Features date palm oases recognized by the FAO as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System (GIAHS) and contains UNESCO heritage sites (e.g., Hili archaeological site).
- **Landmarks:** Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Emirates Palace, Al Maqta Museum, Heritage Village, and Al Ain National Museum.
- **Theme Parks:** Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld, Emirates Park Zoo, and Al Ain Zoo.

### 4.2 Dubai

- **Landmarks & Sites:** Burj Khalifa ('At the Top'), Dubai Frame, Historic Al Fahidi district (Al Bastakiya), and the Jumeirah archaeological site.
- **Theme Parks:** Dubai Parks & Resorts, LegoLand, IMG Worlds of Adventure, Wild Wadi, Aquaventure, and KidZania.
- **Nature:** Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve, Ras Al Khor Wildlife Sanctuary, and Dubai Safari Park.

### 4.3 Sharjah & Other Emirates

- **Sharjah:** Heart of Sharjah, Mleiha archaeological site, Al Montazah theme park, and nature reserves (Wadi Al Helou, Wasit).
- **Ajman:** Ajman Museum, Masfout Castle, Masfout Gate, and The Red Fort.
- **Ras Al Khaimah:** Beautiful coastlines and Saqr Park.
- **Umm Al Quwain:** Dreamland Aqua Park.

### 4.4 Shopping & Festivals

- **Shopping Festivals:** Dubai Shopping Festival, Dubai Summer Surprises, Gitex Shopper, Sharjah Summer Shopping Festival, and White Wednesday / Black Friday (November).
- **Night Markets:** Run extensively during the holy month of Ramadan.
- **Duty-Free:** Available at airports (Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, RAK, Fujairah) and online.
- **Global Village:** Open from November to April, offering shopping, dining, and family entertainment.

## 5. Pre-Travel Checks & Customs Regulations

### 5.1 Preliminary Travel Checks

- **Machine-Readable Passports:** Ensure the passport is machine-readable; many countries reject travelers without them.
- **Travel Bans:** If you have pending legal cases in the UAE, you may have a travel ban. Enquire via the Dubai Police app, smart police stations, or the UAE Public Prosecution before booking travel.

### 5.2 Customs Regulations

- **Declarations:** Travelers must declare cash or goods that exceed duty-free limits to airport officials.
- **Clearance:** Always provide clear and correct information regarding personal goods and travel plans when questioned by customs officials. Follow both the destination country's regulations and UAE customs rules upon return.
