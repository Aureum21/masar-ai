## 1. Visas, Entry Requirements & Transit

### 1.1 Visa on Arrival & Pre-Arranged Visas

Visa regulations for Abu Dhabi fall under the UAE Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP).

- **GCC Nationals:** Do not require a visa and can enter the emirate using a valid GCC national ID or passport.
- **Visa on Arrival:** Many nationalities (e.g., UK, USA, EU, Australia, Canada, Japan, China, etc.) are eligible for a free 30-day or 90-day multiple-entry visa on arrival. Passports must be valid for at least six months from the date of entry.
- **Pre-Arranged Visas:** Nationalities not eligible for Visa on Arrival must arrange a tourist visa in advance through airlines (e.g., Etihad Airways), hotels, or travel agencies via the ICP portal (icp.gov.ae).

### 1.2 Transit & Stopovers

Zayed International Airport (AUH) is the primary gateway, operating as the hub for Etihad Airways.

- **Transit Visas:** Transit visitors can apply for 48-hour or 96-hour transit visas (depending on nationality) through their airline.
- **Proximity:** Major attractions like Yas Island (Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World) and the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque are located just 15–20 minutes from the airport, making them ideal for short layovers.

## 2. Practical Destination Essentials

### 2.1 Currency & Banking

- **Currency:** UAE Dirham (AED or Dhs), divided into 100 fils.
- **Exchange Rate:** Fixed to the US Dollar at $1 USD = 3.6725 AED.
- **Payments:** Credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Maestro, Cirrus) are widely accepted at shops, hotels, and restaurants. Smaller bank notes are preferred when shopping at traditional souks.
- **ATMs & Exchange:** ATMs are located in all malls, petrol stations, and the airport. Bureau de Change offices typically offer competitive rates and operate Saturday to Thursday, 8 AM – 1 PM and 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM (often longer in malls).

### 2.2 Climate & What to Pack

- **Summer (April to September):** Hot and arid. Pack lightweight, breathable fabrics, swimwear, high-protection sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses.
- **Winter (October to March):** Cooler and pleasant, ideal for outdoor activities. Bring a light jacket or sweater for evenings.
- **Indoor Packing:** Air conditioning in malls, cinemas, and hotels can be fierce; packing a pashmina, shawl, or light jacket is highly recommended year-round.

### 2.3 Culture, Etiquette & Dress Code

Abu Dhabi maintains a strong Islamic heritage. While welcoming, visitors should respect local customs:

- **Dress Code:** Clothing should be modest. Shoulders and knees must be covered when visiting government offices, cultural sites, and religious landmarks. Short or tight clothing in public spaces may attract unwelcome attention.
- **Beaches & Pools:** Regular swimwear (shorts, bikinis, full-piece swimsuits) is completely acceptable at private and public beaches/pools. Cover up when leaving the beach area.
- **Alcohol:** Alcohol is available for purchase and consumption in licensed areas, primarily within hotel bars, restaurants, and lounges. You must be over 21 to consume alcohol. Drinking in public spaces (outside of licensed venues) is strictly prohibited.
- **Greetings:** Emirati greetings are warm. However, physical interaction between men and women is conservative. Do not initiate a handshake with an Emirati of the opposite gender unless they offer their hand first. A smile is universally acceptable.
- **Ramadan:** Dress more conservatively and avoid eating, drinking, or smoking in public spaces during daylight hours to respect those fasting.

### 2.4 Emergency Contacts

- **Police:** 999
- **Ambulance:** 998 | **Air Ambulance:** 999
- **Fire Emergency:** 997
- **Tourism Police:** +971 2 800 2626 or +971 2 512 7777 (For general tourist inquiries and non-emergencies).
- **Medical Emergencies:** Sheikh Khalifa Medical City (+971 2 819 0000) and Mediclinic Al Noor Hospital (800 2000) both have excellent Accident & Emergency units. Emergency stabilization treatment is free.

## 3. Public Transport & Getting Around

### 3.1 Public Buses & Hafilat Smart Card

Abu Dhabi’s public buses are modern, air-conditioned, and operate day and night.

- **Hafilat Smart Card:** Cash or bank cards are **not accepted** on buses. Passengers must purchase and load a _Hafilat_ smart card in advance. Day passes and long-term passes are available.
- **Free Travel:** Bus travel is complimentary for senior citizens (aged 60+) and People of Determination.
- **DARB:** The official source and app for Abu Dhabi public transport routes, fares, and service updates.

### 3.2 Taxis & Ride-Hailing

- Taxis are strictly metered, easily hailed off the street, or booked via the Abu Dhabi Taxi app.
- Ride-hailing apps like Uber and Careem are highly popular and widely used across the emirate.

### 3.3 Experience Abu Dhabi Shuttle Bus

- **Overview:** A fleet of colorful, air-conditioned, and Wi-Fi-enabled shuttle buses offering **free** transportation to major landmarks.
- **Routes:** Operates across 8 key routes connecting Abu Dhabi city center, Yas Island, Jubail Island, Saadiyat Island, and the Grand Canal area.
- **Al Ain Shuttle:** Dedicated routes connecting Al Ain Oasis, Al Jahili Fort, Al Ain Zoo, and the Camel Market.
- _(Note: Service routes are periodically updated or paused for seasonal adjustments; visitors should check the app/website for real-time schedules)._

## 5. Explore Abu Dhabi: Key Regions & Islands

### 5.1 Abu Dhabi City & The Corniche

The urban heart of the emirate featuring striking modern architecture alongside heritage sites.

- **The Corniche:** A pristine 8km waterfront featuring pedestrian pathways, cafes, and Corniche Beach.
- **Qasr Al Hosn:** The oldest standing structure in Abu Dhabi (over 200 years old), offering deep insights into the city’s history.
- **Qasr Al Watan:** The stunning working Presidential Palace, renowned for its majestic Arabian architecture and library.
- **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque:** An architectural masterpiece accommodating up to 40,000 worshippers, featuring 82 domes, 24-carat gold chandeliers, and the world's largest hand-knotted carpet.

### 5.2 Saadiyat Island (Cultural District)

Abu Dhabi’s global hub for art, culture, and pristine beaches, located 15 minutes from downtown.

- **Louvre Abu Dhabi:** A spectacular universal museum boasting a famous "rain of light" dome.
- **Upcoming/Current Institutions:** Manarat Al Saadiyat, teamLab Phenomena, Zayed National Museum, Natural History Museum, and Guggenheim Abu Dhabi.
- **Leisure:** Mamsha Al Saadiyat (beachfront dining) and Saadiyat Beach Club.

### 5.3 Yas Island (Entertainment Hub)

The ultimate destination for thrill-seekers and families, located near Zayed International Airport.

- **Theme Parks:** Ferrari World Abu Dhabi (home to Formula Rossa, the world’s fastest rollercoaster), Warner Bros. World™ Abu Dhabi, Yas Waterworld, and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi.
- **Adventure:** CLYMB™ Abu Dhabi (world’s tallest indoor skydiving flight chamber and indoor climbing wall).
- **Motorsports:** Yas Marina Circuit, home to the Formula 1 Etihad Airways Abu Dhabi Grand Prix.

### 5.4 Al Ain (The Oasis City)

A UNESCO World Heritage destination located a 90-minute drive from Abu Dhabi city, acting as the heritage heartland of the emirate.

- **Al Ain Oasis:** 1,200 hectares of lush palm plantations featuring traditional _falaj_ irrigation systems.
- **Historical Forts:** Qasr Al Muwaiji and Al Jahili Fort.
- **Nature & Adventure:** Jebel Hafit (the UAE's second-highest peak, ideal for hiking and stargazing at the Desert Park), Al Ain Zoo, and Al Ain Adventure (the region’s first man-made white-water rafting facility).
- **Culture:** The bustling Al Ain Camel Market.

### 5.5 Al Dhafra

The massive western region comprising over 70% of the emirate, where the desert meets the sea.

- **Vibe:** A place for space, stillness, and deep desert escapes.
- **Key Sites:** Al Dhafra Fort (built in 2017 to honor tribal history), the towering dunes of the Empty Quarter (Rub' al Khali), and luxury desert retreats like Qasr Al Sarab.
