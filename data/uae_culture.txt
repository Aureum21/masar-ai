=== LOUVRE ABU DHABI ===
Location: Saadiyat Cultural District, Abu Dhabi
Operating Hours: 
- Tuesday to Sunday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM (midnight)
- Monday: CLOSED
- Museum Galleries close at 6:30 PM on weekdays and 8:30 PM on weekends (Friday-Sunday).
Tickets: General Admission is AED 63. Under 18 years, UAE residents over 60, and People of Determination enter free.
Dress Code: Smart casual. Shoulders and knees should be covered as a sign of respect.
Highlights: The "Rain of Light" dome, global art history exhibits from prehistory to modern times.

=== SHEIKH ZAYED GRAND MOSQUE ===
Location: Sheikh Rashid Bin Saeed Street, Abu Dhabi
Operating Hours: 
- Saturday to Thursday: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Friday: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, then 3:00 PM - 10:00 PM
Tickets: Entry is completely FREE, but pre-registration online is highly recommended.
Dress Code: STRICTLY ENFORCED. 
- Women: Long, loose-fitting ankle-length clothing, long sleeves covering wrists. Headscarf (Shayla) covering all hair is mandatory.
- Men: Long trousers (no shorts). Shoulders must be covered.
Highlights: One of the world's largest mosques, featuring 82 domes, 24-carat gold gilded chandeliers, and the world's largest hand-knotted carpet.

=== QASR AL WATAN ===
Location: Al Ras Al Akhdar, Abu Dhabi
Operating Hours: Daily from 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM (Palace in Motion light show at 7:30 PM). Timings subject to change due to official state events.
Tickets: General Admission is AED 65 for adults, AED 30 for children.
Dress Code: Modest clothing. Shoulders and knees must be covered.
Highlights: The Presidential Palace of the UAE, showcasing Arabian architectural craftsmanship, the Great Hall, and the House of Knowledge.

=== DUBAI FRAME ===
Location: Zabeel Park, Dubai
Operating Hours: Daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Best time to visit is during sunset.
Tickets: AED 50 for adults, AED 20 for children. Free for infants under 3 and People of Determination with 2 companions.
Dress Code: Casual and respectful.
Highlights: A 150-meter-tall golden frame providing views of Old Dubai to the north and New Dubai to the south, featuring a glass-bottom sky deck.

=== BURJ KHALIFA (AT THE TOP) ===
Location: Downtown Dubai (Enter through Dubai Mall)
Operating Hours: Daily from 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight). Prime hours (sunset) are typically 4:00 PM to 6:30 PM.
Tickets: Standard 'At The Top' (Levels 124 & 125) starts at AED 179. Prime hours are more expensive (AED 259+). 'At The Top SKY' (Level 148) starts at AED 399.
Dress Code: Smart casual.
Highlights: The tallest building in the world (828 meters).

=== AL FAHIDI HISTORICAL NEIGHBOURHOOD ===
Location: Bur Dubai, Dubai
Operating Hours: Open 24/7, but museums and cafes typically open 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Tickets: Free to walk around. Museums inside (like the Coffee Museum or Coin Museum) may have nominal fees or be free.
Dress Code: Modest and casual. Good walking shoes recommended.
Highlights: Traditional wind-tower (barjeel) architecture, narrow alleyways, art galleries, and the Sheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding (SMCCU).
