=== SECTION 1: Major Attractions ===

--- Burj Khalifa & At The Top SKY ---
Location: Downtown Dubai (Enter through Dubai Mall)
Operating Hours: 8:00 AM - 12:00 AM daily
Ticket Prices: 'At The Top' (L124/125) AED 179+, 'SKY' (L148) AED 399+
Dress Code: Smart casual
Key Highlights: Tallest building in the world (828m), outdoor observation deck, incredible sunset views.
Nearest Metro: Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall Station (Red Line)
Best Time to Visit: 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM for sunset (book in advance).

--- Dubai Mall & Dubai Fountain ---
Location: Downtown Dubai
Operating Hours: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM (Mon-Thu), 10:00 AM - 1:00 AM (Fri-Sun)
Ticket Prices: Free to enter. Fountain shows are free.
Dress Code: Modest casual (shoulders and knees covered).
Key Highlights: One of the largest malls globally, Dubai Aquarium (paid entry), Olympic-size ice rink. Fountain shows run every 30 mins from 6:00 PM to 11:00 PM.
Nearest Metro: Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall Station (Red Line)

--- Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque ---
Location: Sheikh Rashid Bin Saeed Street, Abu Dhabi
Operating Hours: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Sat-Thu), 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM & 3:00 PM - 10:00 PM (Fri)
Ticket Prices: Free (Pre-registration online required)
Dress Code: STRICT. Women: long sleeves, ankle-length, headscarf mandatory. Men: long trousers, shoulders covered. No transparent clothing.
Key Highlights: 82 domes, 24-carat gold chandeliers, world's largest hand-knotted carpet.

--- Louvre Abu Dhabi ---
Location: Saadiyat Cultural District, Abu Dhabi
Operating Hours: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM (Tue-Sun), CLOSED Mondays. Galleries close earlier.
Ticket Prices: AED 63 for adults. Under 18 free.
Dress Code: Smart casual.
Key Highlights: "Rain of Light" architecture, global art from prehistory to modern times.

--- Qasr Al Watan ---
Location: Al Ras Al Akhdar, Abu Dhabi
Operating Hours: 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM (Palace in Motion light show at 7:30 PM).
Ticket Prices: AED 65 adults, AED 30 children.
Dress Code: Modest clothing.
Key Highlights: UAE Presidential Palace, Great Hall, House of Knowledge.

--- Dubai Frame ---
Location: Zabeel Park, Dubai
Operating Hours: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM daily.
Ticket Prices: AED 50 adults, AED 20 children.
Dress Code: Casual.
Key Highlights: 150m tall golden frame, views of Old Dubai vs New Dubai, glass-bottom sky deck.
Nearest Metro: Max Station (Red Line)

--- Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood ---
Location: Bur Dubai, Dubai
Operating Hours: 24/7 (Museums 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM)
Ticket Prices: Free to walk around.
Dress Code: Modest and casual.
Key Highlights: Traditional wind-tower architecture, art galleries, SMCCU (Sheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding).
Nearest Metro: Sharaf DG Station (Green Line)

--- Yas Island ---
Location: Abu Dhabi
Key Highlights: Ferrari World (fastest rollercoaster), Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros World, Yas Marina Circuit (F1).
Ticket Prices: Varies per park (approx AED 345+ each, multi-park passes available).

--- Global Village ---
Location: Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road, Dubai
Operating Hours: Seasonal (October to April only). Usually 4:00 PM - 12:00 AM.
Ticket Prices: AED 25-30.
Key Highlights: International pavilions, cultural shows, street food, shopping.

--- Ras Al Khaimah Jebel Jais ---
Location: Ras Al Khaimah (approx 2 hours from Dubai)
Key Highlights: Highest mountain in the UAE (1,934m). Features Jais Flight (world's longest zipline, AED 300+). Temperatures are typically 10 degrees cooler than sea level.

--- Hatta Mountain Resort & Hatta Dam ---
Location: Hatta (Dubai exclave in Hajar Mountains, ~90 mins from Dubai)
Key Highlights: Kayaking in Hatta Dam, mountain biking, hiking, Hatta Heritage Village.

=== SECTION 2: UAE Visa Information ===

Visa on Arrival:
- 30-Day Visa: Citizens of US, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, and most EU/Western nations receive a free 30-day visa on arrival.
- 90-Day Visa: Citizens of many EU nations, South Korea, Argentina, etc.
- GCC Citizens: Freedom of movement, no visa required.

Tourist Visas (Pre-arranged):
- Available for 30 or 60 days. Can be arranged via airlines (Emirates/Etihad), hotels, or travel agencies.
- Cost: approx AED 300-500 depending on duration.

Overstay Fines:
- AED 50 per day for overstaying (plus exit fees). Strictly enforced.

=== SECTION 3: Cultural Guidelines & Laws ===

Dress Code in Public:
- Malls/Public areas: Shoulders and knees should be covered. Avoid overly tight or transparent clothing.
- Beaches/Pools: Swimwear is acceptable at beaches and pools, but must be covered when leaving the beach area. No topless sunbathing.
- Mosques: Strict modesty. Women must cover hair, arms, and legs completely. Men must wear long trousers.

Public Behavior:
- No Public Displays of Affection (PDA) beyond holding hands (for married couples).
- Offensive language, swearing, and rude gestures (including driving) are illegal and can lead to fines/deportation.
- Photography: Do not photograph government buildings, military sites, or people (especially local women) without explicit permission.

Alcohol & Drugs:
- Alcohol is available in licensed hotels, bars, and clubs to non-Muslims over 21. Drinking in public spaces or being intoxicated in public is illegal.
- Zero tolerance for illegal drugs. Even trace amounts in bloodstream or luggage can result in severe prison sentences.

Ramadan Guidelines:
- Eating, drinking, smoking, and chewing gum in public during daylight hours is strictly prohibited for everyone, regardless of religion.
- Hotels usually have screened-off dining areas for non-Muslim tourists during the day.

Drones & VPNs:
- Drones: Illegal to fly without GCAA and local police permits. High fines for violations.
- VPNs: Legal for corporate use, but illegal if used to commit a crime, hide IP for fraud, or access blocked content (like VoIP calling apps like WhatsApp/FaceTime calls, which are blocked in the UAE).

=== SECTION 4: UAE Transportation ===

Dubai Metro:
- Red Line (runs along Sheikh Zayed Road, connects Airport, Burj Khalifa, Marina).
- Green Line (connects Old Dubai areas like Deira and Bur Dubai).
- Timings: 5:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight) Mon-Thu. Fridays 5:00 AM to 1:00 AM. Sundays 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM.
- Nol Card: Reusable smart card required for payment. Fares range from AED 3 to AED 7.50 per trip.
- Note: Eating, drinking (even water), or chewing gum on the Metro is fined (AED 100). Gold Class available for premium fare.

Taxis & Ride-Hailing:
- RTA Taxis (Cream color with colored roofs): AED 12 starting fare, AED 2.14 per km. Airport pickup starting fare is AED 25.
- Careem and Uber: Widely used. Careem is the local dominant app (acquired by Uber) and can also book RTA Hala Taxis.

Inter-Emirate Travel:
- Dubai to Abu Dhabi: E11 Highway. ~90-120 minutes by car. Taxi costs ~AED 250-300. Intercity buses (E100/E101) run frequently from Al Ghubaiba/Ibn Battuta for AED 25.
- Dubai to Sharjah: Extremely heavy traffic during rush hours. Distance is short but can take 45-90 mins.
- Etihad Rail: National railway (currently freight, passenger services coming soon).

Road Tolls & Parking:
- Salik (Dubai Toll): AED 4 automatically deducted when passing under a toll gate (e.g., Sheikh Zayed Road). Rental cars pass this cost to the renter.
- Darb (Abu Dhabi Toll): Active during peak hours.
- Parking: Paid via RTA app, SMS, or machines. Free on Sundays and public holidays.

=== SECTION 5: Weather & Best Times to Visit ===

Winter (November to March) - Best Time:
- Temps: 20°C to 28°C.
- Ideal for outdoor activities, desert safaris, and beach days. Peak tourist season.

Summer (May to September) - Extreme Heat:
- Temps: 38°C to 45°C+ (100°F - 115°F+). High humidity in July/August.
- Outdoor activities are heavily restricted. Beaches and pools are only usable early morning or after sunset.
- Focus strictly on indoor, air-conditioned activities (malls, indoor theme parks, museums).
- Current Month: August 2026 is peak summer.

Rainfall:
- Rare, averaging 5 days a year, mostly between December and March. Can cause temporary flooding.

=== SECTION 6: Dubai Summer Surprises (DSS) 2026 ===

- The UAE's biggest summer festival running through August 2026.
- Offers massive retail discounts (up to 75% off) across major malls (Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, City Centre).
- 10 Dirham Dish: Special AED 10 signature dishes at participating restaurants across Dubai from August 3 to August 30.
- Kids Go Free: Many hotels and attractions offer free entry/stays for children during DSS.
- Indoor entertainment, live concerts, and daily raffles.

=== SECTION 7: Currency & Payments ===

- Currency: UAE Dirham (AED). Fixed peg: 1 USD = 3.6725 AED.
- Payments: Highly cashless society. Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa, and Mastercard accepted almost everywhere (even small groceries and taxis).
- Tipping: Not mandatory but appreciated. 10-15% in restaurants (check if service charge is already included).
- VAT: 5%. Tourists can claim VAT refunds on shopping >AED 250 at Planet Tax Free kiosks at the airport before departure.

=== SECTION 8: Practical Information ===

- Emergency Numbers: Police (999), Ambulance (998), Fire (997), Tourist Police (800 2626).
- Language: Arabic is official, but English is the lingua franca and used everywhere.
- Weekend: Saturday and Sunday are the official weekend. Friday midday is for congregational prayers (some smaller shops may close 12pm-2pm).
- Power: 220-240V, Type G (UK 3-pin) plugs.
- Internet/SIM: Etisalat (e&) and Du. Tourist SIMs available at immigration/baggage claim (sometimes handed out for free with 1GB data). Excellent 5G coverage nationwide.
- Water: Tap water is desalinated and safe to drink, though most locals and tourists prefer bottled water.
- Healthcare: World-class private and government hospitals. Travel insurance is highly recommended as private healthcare is expensive.
