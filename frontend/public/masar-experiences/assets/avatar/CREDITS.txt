Modifications in this project: bust trimmed; coordinates and scale adapted;
skin material tinted; normal mapping configured; eyes, brows, fitted beard,
ghutra and agal added. The source scan depicts its own person. It is not a
3D reconstruction or exact likeness of the supplied reference photograph.

Garment mesh, clothing rig, headwear, beard and animation integration authored
for this local Masar demonstration. Three.js and its GLTFLoader / geometry
